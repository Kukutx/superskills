# Platforms

Routes, entry points, content types and approximate limits. Limits change: check the platform's page at publish time and follow what it shows. Do not hard-code a limit into a brief.

## Connected route

Publish through the connected account. Web login is a backup only.

- Instagram: video, image, carousel. Vertical 9:16 first. Caption and hashtags go in one block.
- Facebook: video, image, text, link. First paragraph carries the topic.
- Threads: text, image, video. Keep copy short and put the conclusion first.

## Browser route

Use the user's logged-in browser for every platform in this section. Never use a browser that does not share the user's logins.

- YouTube: entry is YouTube Studio at https://studio.youtube.com/. Video only. Fill title, description and tags in separate fields. Wait for processing to finish. Declare audience and originality as asked.
- TikTok: entry is TikTok Studio at https://www.tiktok.com/tiktokstudio/. Video only. Copy acts as the title and hashtags go in the copy. Vertical 9:16 first. Complete the content declaration items.
- X: entry is https://x.com/. Text, image, video. Copy stays in one post and the media attaches to it. Split long copy only after the user confirms.
- Bilibili: entry is the Creator Center at https://member.bilibili.com/platform/home. Video only. Choose a category before upload. Fill title, description, tags and cover. Horizontal 16:9 is the main format.
- Xiaohongshu: entry is the Creator Center at https://creator.rednote.com/new/home. Do not use creator.xiaohongshu.com, which redirects to SMS login. Video, image, text. Title stays short, body is separate, cover affects clicks. Put hashtags at the end of the body.
- LinkedIn: entry is https://www.linkedin.com/. Text, image, video, link. Write for a professional reader, keep copy complete and restrained. Few hashtags at the end. Product and work related content fits best here.

## Private and community route

These three are not public publishing platforms. They need a named destination in every request, and they are never included in all-platform mode. Do not publish to a private chat, a personal group or a customer service chat unless the user names that exact destination in that request.

- WhatsApp: entry is WhatsApp Web at https://web.whatsapp.com/. Video, image, text. Use a Channel for broadcast, a group for a community, or Status for a 24-hour update. Status is vertical 9:16, about 60 to 90 seconds, about 10 MB through the web (about 16 MB in the app). Status is best posted from the phone; the web route is limited.
- Discord: entry is https://discord.com/app. Video, image, text, file. Post the content as a message in one server channel, never to a public feed. Free accounts are limited to 20 MB per file.
- Telegram: entry is Telegram Web at https://web.telegram.org/. Video, image, text, file. Post to one channel or group. Free accounts can send up to 2 GB per file.

## Copy limits (approximate)

When copy exceeds a limit, shorten it first and publish only after the user confirms the change.

- X: one post about 280 characters. Longer copy needs a thread or a shorter version.
- Threads: one post about 500 characters.
- Instagram caption: about 2,200 characters, hashtags count in it.
- Telegram caption on a video or image: about 1,024 characters. A text message can be about 4,096 characters.
- Discord message: about 2,000 characters per message.
- LinkedIn post: about 3,000 characters.
- YouTube title: about 100 characters. Bilibili title: about 80 characters. Xiaohongshu title: about 20 characters.
- Tags: at most 10 per post.
