# Personal Voice

## Scope

Use for any text written for or as one specific person: notes, articles, interview material, posts in their name. The person's voice guide is configuration the user supplies: tone, what to include and leave out, fixed public wording, sourcing rules, conventions of their note library, the languages they maintain, and the checklist to run before delivering. The guide wins over general writing habits. A brand voice guide belongs to `writing/brand-voice-guide`; a professional email belongs to `writing/business-email`.

## Rules that hold for any person

- Quote the person's own words verbatim and mark them as quotes; never present material compiled by an AI as the person's words.
- Do not supply reasons the person never gave; mark them as not stated.
- Write only what the person actually thinks and did; when unsure, mark the sentence for them to confirm.
- When the text is kept in several languages, follow the guide's languages section, and the multilingual-docs skill when it is installed.
- Run the guide's checklist before delivering.

## Before delivering

- Every quoted line traces to the person's own words; every compiled or paraphrased line is marked as such, not presented as a quote.
- Every reason given is one the person gave; anything else is marked not stated.
- Every sentence the writer is unsure about is marked for confirmation, not smoothed over.
- Fixed public wording from the guide is used as written, not reworded.
- The guide's checklist has been run, and anything it flags is fixed or marked.
