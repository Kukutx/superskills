# Business Email

## Workflow

1. Identify the action/result the email should produce.
2. Keep only context the recipient needs to act correctly.
3. Put the ask, decision or next step where it cannot be missed.
4. Match tone to the relationship and risk: friendly, neutral, firm, apologetic, etc.
5. Preserve the user's position; do not add concessions, admissions or promises they did not authorize.
6. Edit for natural language, not corporate formality.

## Output

Normally provide the **send-ready email** directly, with a subject when useful.

Add a shorter alternative or tone note only when it materially helps. Do not automatically produce multiple versions.

## Constraints

- Do not invent dates, facts, attachments, agreements or prior conversations.
- Avoid over-apologizing, filler greetings and inflated corporate phrases.
- Do not weaken a request by burying it in excessive politeness.
- Sensitive/disputed matters should use factual, neutral wording rather than unnecessary admissions of liability.
- Preserve important nuance from the user's source text instead of rewriting the meaning for smoothness.
- Follow the language and level of formality requested by the user/recipient context.
