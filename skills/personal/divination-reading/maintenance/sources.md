# Divination sources

Maintenance-only. These sources support historical/calendrical/method boundaries. They do **not** establish the predictive validity of divination or astrology.

## High-trust reference anchors

- Hong Kong Observatory Almanac / calendar resources — traditional Chinese calendar, 24 solar terms, time zones, solar transit and apparent-solar-time concepts. Useful when checking calendrical/time claims rather than relying on a fortune-telling website.
  - https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/astron2026/almanac2026_index.htm
  - https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/time/24solarterms.htm
- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, “Chinese Philosophy of Change (Yijing)” — history, 64-hexagram divination context and philosophical interpretation of the Changes.
  - https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/chinese-change/
- Metropolitan Museum of Art, Tarot history/structure — historical 78-card structure and the later development of occult/fortune-telling associations.
  - https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/tarot-2
- Oxford Bibliographies in Hinduism, “Astrology” by Martin Gansten — scholarly overview of Jyotiṣa terminology and branches such as jātaka, muhūrta and praśna.
  - https://academic.oup.com/reference/62357/reference-article-abstract/554513393

## Traditional text families

Use these as historical/traditional interpretive sources, **not scientific evidence**. Editions, commentaries and school lineages vary.

- Yijing / Zhouyi and commentarial traditions
- 《三命通会》
- 《滴天髓》 and commentarial traditions
- 《子平真诠》
- 《渊海子平》
- 《增删卜易》 / 《卜筮正宗》 for 六爻 traditions
- 《梅花易数》 tradition
- 奇门遁甲、六壬、太乙 classical/commentarial traditions
- Jyotiṣa classical and modern scholarly translations appropriate to the branch being used

Do not treat a single modern website, chart calculator or social-media school as the universal authority for all lineages.

## Runtime verification policy

When exact calculation matters, re-check current or authoritative data at execution time:

```text
civil date/time
historical timezone / DST
coordinates
solar-term boundary / calendar conversion
ephemeris / planetary positions
ayanāṁśa / house-system convention
charting-school convention
```

A source can establish a calendar or astronomical input without validating the divinatory interpretation built on top of it.

## Source quality rule

Prefer:

```text
astronomical/calendar institution
-> academic/history reference
-> primary/traditional text or reputable edition
-> specialist secondary commentary
-> popular calculator/blog only as a convenience, cross-checked when exactness matters
```

Never copy large tables of interpretations into runtime references merely to look comprehensive. Preserve the decision rules that prevent wrong charting and incoherent method mixing.
